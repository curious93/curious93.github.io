Sun per Euro

Where is the most warmth for your money this winter?

A decision tool for winter travellers without a fixed destination. Pick your departure airport and month; we rank every non-stop destination by how much warmer it is per euro of airfare.

In development · Launch winter 2026/27

What it does

Most travel sites sort by price. Sun per Euro sorts by the reason people fly south in winter: warmth. For each departure airport and month we combine airfares with destination weather and show which trips give the most extra degrees per euro.

How it works

1. ChooseDeparture airport in Germany, travel month from November to March, trip length and luggage.
2. CompareTop 10 destinations ranked by euro per degree, with sunshine hours, rainy days and a seasonal trend.
3. BookLinks go to established flight and hotel booking partners. Prices are checked there before you book.

Independence

Sun per Euro is an independent project from Germany. Booking links lead to established flight, hotel and package partners and are labelled as advertising. Rankings are computed from weather and price only and are never influenced by commission.
